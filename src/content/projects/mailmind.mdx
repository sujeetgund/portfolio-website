## Taming Inbox Chaos with Event-Driven AI Agents

Knowledge workers spend over 2 hours daily dealing with email overload. Traditional email filters rely on rigid keyword regexes that lack semantic context, resulting in missed critical inquiries, repetitive manual drafting, and high operational fatigue.

**MailMind** is an event-driven email processing and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) orchestration system built on **LangGraph**, **FastAPI**, **Resend Inbound Webhooks**, **FAISS**, and **Next.js 16**. It automatically ingests incoming emails, classifies intent, matches natural-language business rules, generates RAG-grounded responses, and pauses for human approval on high-stakes decisions.

## Technical Deep-Dive into Core Systems

### 1. Resend Inbound Webhooks & Svix Security

Every incoming email triggers an HTTP POST request to `/api/webhook`. MailMind verifies the Svix cryptographic signature to prevent webhook spoofing before fetching the complete body and attachments via the Resend Receiving API.

### 2. Pydantic Structured Intent Routing

Using `ChatGroq` with tool-calling enforcement, MailMind classifies emails into 6 distinct categories and extracts structured JSON metadata:

- **Categories**: `Invoice`, `Support Query`, `Meeting Request`, `Spam`, `Urgent Alert`, `General`
- **Extracted Attributes**: Sender identity, monetary values, dates, urgency rating (scale 1-10), and key action items.

### 3. Stateful Graph Checkpointing & Resilient HITL Queue

Built on **LangGraph `StateGraph`** with a **PostgreSQL Checkpointer**:

- When an email triggers high-urgency conditions (Urgency Score >= 8 or monetary value > $500), the graph executes a native `interrupt()` call.
- The execution state is frozen and saved to PostgreSQL.
- The Next.js dashboard receives a real-time SSE notification displaying the email context, extracted metadata, proposed action, and draft reply.
- Once the human operator clicks **Approve** or **Modify**, the graph resumes execution from the exact checkpoint and dispatches the email response via Resend SDK.

## Key Platform Innovations

- **RAG-Grounded Auto-Replies**: FAISS vector search queries per-user knowledge bases to ground responses in verified company documentation.
- **Plain-English Rule Editor**: Natural-language business rules (e.g., _"If invoice amount exceeds $1000, forward to finance team"_) dynamically stored as JSON schemas and evaluated at runtime.
- **Real-time SSE Dashboard**: Live Server-Sent Events stream node transitions, confidence ratings, and state changes to the frontend in real time.
